DailyShield – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Introduction

DailyShield ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by PSBM Labs. We built DailyShield as an offline-first privacy and productivity toolkit, and we designed it so that your personal data stays on your own device. This Privacy Policy explains what the app accesses, what it stores, what it never collects, and the choices available to you. By installing or using DailyShield, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Our Core Privacy Principle

DailyShield does not require an account, a login, or a sign-up. We do not operate a server that stores your personal content. Every item you create inside the app — receipts, expenses, notes, password reminders, water reminder settings and auto-silent zones — is saved only in the app's private local storage (Hive database) on your device. We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded to us.

3. Information Processed by Each Module

  • Receipt Scanner – You choose a photo from your camera or gallery. Text is extracted using Google ML Kit Text Recognition, which runs fully on-device. A copy of the selected image is stored inside the app's private folder and the extracted text, merchant name, amount, date and category are saved locally. No receipt image or receipt text is uploaded to any server.
  • Expense Tracker – All expense records, totals and categories stay in the local database on your device.
  • App Permission Audit – The app reads the list of applications installed on your device and the permissions they declare, only to display a privacy report to you on screen. This list is generated in real time, is not stored, and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Password Expiry Tracker – This module is a rotation reminder tool. It stores only a label (for example "Email" or "Bank"), a category, an optional note and the date you last changed that password. DailyShield never asks for, stores, or transmits your actual passwords.
  • Water Reminder – This module helps you stay hydrated by sending periodic notifications during your chosen active window. Your reminder settings (start time, end time, interval, daily goal and glass size) are saved locally on your device only. The app logs only the number of glasses you log each day; no personal health or medical data is collected, processed or shared. Reminders use on-device scheduling — no location or personal data is sent to any server.
  • Location Based Auto Silent – When you create a silent zone, the coordinates and radius you select are saved on your device and registered with the Android geofencing service so your phone can switch to silent mode when you enter that area. Your location is used only on your device to trigger this ringer change. We do not log, track, store on a server, or share your location history with anyone.
  • Notebook – Notes you write are stored locally on your device only.

4. Permissions We Request and Why

  • Camera – to capture a receipt photo for scanning.
  • Photos / Storage – to let you pick an existing receipt image and to save the cropped copy inside the app's own folder.
  • Location (approximate, precise and background) – required for the Auto Silent feature. Background location is needed so the geofence can still switch your phone to silent when the app is closed or not in use. This permission is optional; if you do not use Auto Silent, you can deny it and the rest of the app will continue to work.
  • Do Not Disturb access (Notification Policy) – to change your ringer mode to silent and restore it when you leave the zone, and to send water reminder notifications.
  • Notifications – to show foreground service notifications required by Android for geofencing and to send water reminder notifications on schedule.
  • Run at startup and Wake lock – so your saved silent zones and water reminder schedule keep working after the device restarts.
  • Internet and Network state – to load advertisements, check connectivity and send a message if you use the Contact Us form.

We do not access your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, or microphone.

5. Contact Form

If you voluntarily use the Contact Us screen, the name, email address, subject and message you type are sent through the EmailJS delivery service so that our support team can reply to you. This information is used only to answer your query and is not used for marketing or sold to anyone. If you prefer not to share these details, simply do not use the form and email us directly instead.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

DailyShield is free and is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language and general region
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy practices: policies.google.com/privacy

You can review or withdraw your ad personalization consent at any time using the "Manage Ad Privacy Choices" button on this screen.

The app also uses Google Play In-App Review to optionally ask for a rating, and Google Fonts to load typefaces.

7. Your Consent Choices (GDPR / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown when you first open the app, in line with GDPR and UK data protection requirements. Your choice can be changed at any time from this screen.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your content is stored only on your device, you remain in full control of it. You may delete any individual receipt, expense, note, password reminder, water reminder log or silent zone from inside the app. Uninstalling DailyShield removes the entire local database and all saved receipt images from your device permanently. We hold no backup copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered by us or by you.

9. Children's Privacy

DailyShield is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age applicable in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information through the contact form, please write to us and we will delete it.

10. Data Security

Your records are kept in the app's private storage area, which other applications cannot read on a non-rooted device. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect any data passing through the app. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we recommend keeping a screen lock enabled on your device.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data held about you. Since DailyShield does not store your personal data on any server, these rights are exercised directly on your device. For data collected by our advertising partner, please use the Google link in Section 6.

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of its sale or sharing for advertising purposes. DailyShield does not sell or share personal data; such requests should be directed to Google using the link in Section 6.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any change will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
